# Athlete Identity — owner iPhone acceptance

Open START-IXHUA.bat on your computer, scan its QR on the same Wi-Fi, and use Safari. The app is the actual accepted checkpoint and local persistence layer. Physical iPhone results start as **NOT RUN**.

For every round: complete the conditional questions → apply your program → inspect Today, Training, Progress and Plan → reload → compare the saved answers and program. Then use **Owner testing → Start fresh test → Reset and restart onboarding** to begin the next round. Download a round before another reset if you want to keep it beyond the single previous-round backup.

| Round | Try | Inspect in the actual app |
|---|---|---|
| Runner | First 5K, current continuous running ability, recent training, three running days, long-run window; different lifting experience | Today and Training lead with running; real timed run/walk segments if appropriate; runs retain priority over support lifts; Running history stays separate from Cardio. Try `9.15` as a benchmark time: it means 9:15. |
| Strength & Muscle | Experienced muscle-building goal, days/time, equipment, muscle priorities and benchmark loads | Lifting-first Today/Training/Progress; working sets, split and available exercises reflect those answers. Compare to a new lifter with three shorter sessions. |
| Hybrid | Separate running and lifting experience; choose running-first, strength-first or equal; shared availability | One coordinated calendar; protected priority and explicit compromises when time is constrained. Benchmark entries accept the displayed lb/kg units, including decimals. |
| General Fitness | Realistic days, preferred aerobic activity and movement needs | Balanced weekly strength/aerobic/movement practice and consistency priorities, rather than a renamed race or hypertrophy plan. |
| Longevity | Retain your true lifting experience; enter known balance, chair-stand and walking baselines; name an ability you value | Capability goals and baselines stay visible; exercise support and walking dose respond to capacity. Age does not erase lifting experience. |
| Custom | Select only the activities you want; choose which leads; change that selection in another round | Selected activities determine the actual program and leading Training/Progress domain. Unselected running or lifting should not be silently inserted. |

Also check:

- Reload midway through a question, then after applying your program. Your draft and accepted program should remain.
- Complete a real prescribed run or strength session. Pause, reload, finish and inspect its summary/history. Empty Progress should show useful next actions until you log data.
- Change a profile via **Settings → Edit athlete profile** when you want to retain history. End an active workout before applying a new program.
- Try portrait, landscape, the keyboard, scrolling, larger text and Reduced Motion. Report clipped fields, awkward controls or confusing wording.
- Try the reset cancellation: **Keep this test** must leave answers and logs intact. Reset keeps feedback and a downloadable previous round; unrelated browser data stays untouched.

Open **Owner testing** to record PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or NOT RUN for each journey, enter your iPhone/iOS version and notes, then **Download test record**. Feedback saves automatically on this phone. A useful issue includes the identity, screen, input, expected result and what actually happened. Do not share the temporary QR pairing link or API credentials.

This acceptance does not approve the full RC, live AI/scanning, native Watch/Health/GPS or production. Those retain their explicit status in the build panel and master matrix. No next subsystem will begin while this owner feedback is pending.

# IXHUA 0.1.2 — Hybrid Conditioning Methodology

## Purpose
This build adds an explicit **Core + Athletic Conditioning** decision to Hybrid onboarding. The user may choose General Athletic Conditioning, Combat Sports / Mixed Demands, MMA, Grappling, or Striking. This is a separate hard-conditioning session from low-intensity Zone 2 finishers.

## Evidence basis
IXHUA treats combat-sport conditioning as intermittent high-intensity work, not as a single universal "lactate system" workout.

Key evidence reviewed for this implementation:
- Vasconcelos et al. 2020, systematic review + meta-analysis of HIIT in combat sports. PMID **31904713**, DOI 10.1519/JSC.0000000000003255.
- Franchini et al. 2019, systematic review of HIIT in Olympic combat-sport athletes. PMID **30431531**, DOI 10.1519/JSC.0000000000002957.
- Yue et al. 2025, systematic review + meta-analysis of HIIT in Olympic combat sports. PMID **40415789**, DOI 10.3389/fphys.2025.1576676.
- Ojeda-Aravena et al. 2023, systematic review + meta-analysis of plyometric-jump training in combat-sport athletes. PMID **36828318**, DOI 10.3390/sports11020033.
- Wilson et al. 2012, concurrent endurance + resistance training meta-analysis. PMID **22002517**, DOI 10.1519/JSC.0b013e31823a3e2d.
- Murlasits et al. 2018, concurrent strength/endurance sequencing meta-analysis. PMID **28783467**, DOI 10.1080/02640414.2017.1364405.
- 2023 systematic review/meta-analysis on concurrent training by sex and training status. PMID **37847373**.

## Implementation rules
1. **Hybrid means both running and strength remain core.** Dedicated conditioning is additive only when the schedule can support it.
2. **Hard conditioning is not Zone 2.** Zone 2 remains easy, conversational support after suitable strength sessions.
3. **No single work:rest ratio is presented as universally optimal.** The current fan-bike examples are IXHUA programming choices scaled to experience, not scientific laws.
4. **Quality is protected.** Hard conditioning is not stacked blindly beside another high-demand session. The planner protects heavy lower-body, long-run, interval, and combat-conditioning stress.
5. **Plyometrics remain quality-first primers**, generally 1–2 suitable exposures rather than daily fatigue work.
6. **Recovery mobility may occupy an otherwise open recovery day** when explicitly selected. It does not consume a core training slot.
7. Core work favors anti-rotation, carries, trunk control and hip/trunk integration. The app does not claim that one ab exercise is universally "MMA specific."
8. Progression should be driven by repeatable rounds and logged tolerance. The first hard interval should not be so aggressive that output collapses through the session.

## Current conditioning presets
These are starting templates, not immutable rules:
- New: 15 s hard / 45 s easy × 6
- Some experience: 20 s hard / 40 s easy × 10
- Experienced: 30 s hard / 60 s easy × 8
- Advanced: 30 s hard / 60 s easy × 10

Each uses a progressive warm-up and easy cooldown. Future methodology versions should expand sport-specific options and progression using actual logged performance.
